About DiscoverWorld Travel

Crafting extraordinary journeys for curious souls since 2005.

Our Story

DiscoverWorld Travel was founded in 2005 by a group of passionate backpackers who believed that travel is one of the most transformative experiences a person can have. Starting from a small office in Thamel, Kathmandu, we have grown into a globally recognised boutique travel company with partnerships across 150+ destinations worldwide.

Our founders have personally visited every destination we offer. This first-hand knowledge allows us to design authentic, meaningful experiences — not cookie-cutter tours, but genuine adventures that connect you with local culture, landscapes, and people.

Over the past two decades, we have helped more than 50,000 travellers from 60+ countries discover the world's most extraordinary places.

Our Core Values

Everything we do is guided by three pillars: Authenticity — every experience we offer is genuine and thoughtfully designed; Sustainability — we actively support local communities and minimise environmental impact; Excellence — we maintain the highest standards in every interaction, from first enquiry to safe return home.
