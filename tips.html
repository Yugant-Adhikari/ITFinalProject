Essential Travel Tips

Insider advice from seasoned travellers to make every journey smoother, safer, and more memorable.

🧳 Packing Smart

Packing efficiently is an art form. Here are our top recommendations from two decades of travel planning experience:

Roll, Don't Fold

Rolling your clothes instead of folding them saves up to 30% more space in your luggage and also reduces wrinkles significantly.

Pack a 3-1-1 Toiletry Bag

Keep all liquids in containers of 100ml or less, placed in a single transparent zip-lock bag (max 1 litre) for easy airport security screening.

Always Pack a Day Bag

A lightweight backpack or tote is essential for day trips, keeping valuables, water, and essentials at hand without lugging your main case.

Digital Copies of Documents

Photograph or scan your passport, visa, insurance, and tickets. Email them to yourself and store in cloud storage for instant access if originals are lost.

💰 Money & Budget Tips

Notify Your Bank Before Travelling

Alert your bank or card issuer of your travel dates and destinations to avoid having your card blocked for suspicious overseas activity.

Use a No-Fee Travel Credit Card

Cards like the Wise or Revolut card allow you to spend abroad with no foreign transaction fees and excellent exchange rates.

Carry Some Local Currency

Always have some cash in local currency for small vendors, tips, and places that don't accept cards — especially in rural areas.

Budget 10–15% Emergency Buffer

Whatever you plan to spend, add 10–15% more as an emergency buffer for unexpected costs, opportunities, or delays.

🏥 Health & Safety

Get Travel Vaccinations Early

Visit a travel health clinic 6–8 weeks before departure. Vaccinations for hepatitis, typhoid, yellow fever, and malaria prophylaxis may be needed depending on your destination.

Purchase Comprehensive Travel Insurance

Medical evacuation alone from a remote destination can cost $100,000+. Always purchase travel insurance including medical coverage and emergency evacuation.

Stay Hydrated and Watch What You Eat

Drink only bottled or purified water in developing countries. Be cautious with street food initially — let your stomach adjust gradually.

📱 Tech & Apps

Download Offline Maps

Use Google Maps or Maps.me to download offline maps of your destination before leaving. This saves data and works without internet.

Get a Local SIM or International Plan

A local SIM card is usually the cheapest option for data abroad. Compare international roaming plans from your home carrier too.

Use a VPN for Security

Public Wi-Fi in hotels and airports can be insecure. A VPN encrypts your connection and protects your banking and personal data.
