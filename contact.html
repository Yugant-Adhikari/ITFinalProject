Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 3–6 months in advance for peak season travel and popular destinations. For off-peak times, 4–8 weeks is usually sufficient. Contact us and we'll advise you based on your specific destination.

Do your packages include flights?

Most of our packages are land-only (accommodation, transfers, tours). We can arrange flights for you as an add-on. We work with major airlines to find the best fares and will include all flight costs transparently in your quote.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard), and PayPal. A 30% deposit secures your booking, with the balance due 60 days before departure.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations 90+ days before departure receive a full refund minus a $100 admin fee. 60–89 days: 75% refund. 30–59 days: 50% refund. Under 30 days: no refund. We strongly recommend travel insurance.
